# Apogee Reader

Apogee is an iOS app for your own [BookOrbit](https://github.com/bookorbit) library:
audiobooks, ebooks, comics and PDFs, from a server you host.
It's an independent app. It isn't made or endorsed by the BookOrbit project.

**Website: [apogeereader.app](https://apogeereader.app/)**

The app's on TestFlight. Its code is private. This repo is where you get help, report bugs,
and follow what I'm building.

**Apogee runs on iOS 18 and later.**

## Reporting a bug

The app writes most of the report for you. Open **More**, tap **Feedback & Bug Reports**,
then **File on GitHub**. That fills in your build, your iOS version, your device, and the
kind of address your server uses. It never includes your server's name or address.

Then please add the part only you know:

- what you did
- what you expected
- what happened instead

Screenshots and screen recordings help.

Crashed, or something looks wrong on screen? Take a screenshot and use TestFlight's
**Share Beta Feedback** instead. The screenshot goes with it.

Not sure it's a bug? Start a
[discussion](https://github.com/briarrrn/apogee-issues/discussions). I'll open an issue if
it turns out to be one.

## Following development

I work in a private repo. When a fix reaches TestFlight I post it here, so you can see
what changed and check it on your own device. Watch this repo (👁 Watch → All Activity)
to hear about it.
