Apogee Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Apogee is a client app for self-hosted BookOrbit servers. This policy explains how Apogee handles your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of Apogee does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data, and operates no servers that receive your data.

How your data flows

Apogee talks only to the BookOrbit server you choose to connect to, using the address and credentials you enter. Your library, reading and listening progress, highlights, and bookmarks move only between your device and that server. Whoever runs that server decides how your data is stored and protected.

On-device data

Downloaded books, cached covers, and your settings stay on your device. Delete the app and it is all gone.

Diagnostics you choose to share

The app can build a short report about your setup: the app build, your iOS version, your device model, whether the app is online, how many changes are waiting to sync, and how many files you have downloaded. It classifies the kind of address your server uses, such as "Tailscale" or "public domain". It does not include your server's name or address.

The app can also save a file of its own log lines from the last fifteen minutes, with your server's host name removed.

Both are shown to you before anything happens, and neither leaves your device unless you choose to share it. The app sends nothing on its own, and the developer receives nothing you have not sent.

Third parties

Apogee contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
