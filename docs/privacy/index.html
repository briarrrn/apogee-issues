Apogee — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

Apogee is a client app for self-hosted BookOrbit servers. This policy explains how Apogee handles your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of Apogee does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data, and operates no servers that receive your data.

How your data flows

Apogee communicates only with the BookOrbit server you choose to connect to, using the address and credentials you enter. Your library, reading and listening progress, highlights, and bookmarks are exchanged solely between your device and that server. How that data is stored and protected is governed by the operator of the BookOrbit server you connect to.

On-device data

Downloaded books, cached cover images, and your settings are stored locally on your device. You can remove all of it by deleting the app.

Third parties

Apogee contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
